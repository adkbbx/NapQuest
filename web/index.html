      The optional quiet-bell story

      The bell rings every eight seconds. A wax-and-fiber muffler will stop it. The beekeeper has honey wax, across a broken river bridge.

      1. Chop two trees and mine one rock. This gives 4 wood, 2 fiber, and 2 stone.
      2. Repair the crossing with the wood and stone. Cross the river and accept the beekeeper's honey job.
      3. At a workbench, craft the muffler with 2 fiber and 1 honey. Fit it to the village bell.
      4. Enter your hollow tree and take a proper nap. You can keep playing afterward.

      Tap trees, rocks and stocked mines to walk over and gather. Shops, jobs and repairs open choices before spending or accepting. E repeats the selected action. Clicking empty ground walks there. Moving cancels a task.
